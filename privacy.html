Privacy Policy

Visual Acuity Lab (visualacuitylab.com)

Summary

Visual Acuity Lab is a free tool that runs entirely in your web browser. We do not operate user accounts, sell data, or use advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

What we do not collect

We do not ask you to sign in and we do not receive the numbers you enter into the calculator. Calculations run on your device; we have no server that stores your inputs or results.

Data stored on your device

Your browser may save a few small preferences locally (using localStorage): color theme, display language, and whether you accepted the on-site disclaimer. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear it anytime through your browser settings.

Hosting and third parties

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; the host may process standard web server logs (such as IP address and request time) under its own policies. We load fonts from Google Fonts when you use the site. Links such as email support or "Buy me a coffee" open external services with their own privacy practices.
